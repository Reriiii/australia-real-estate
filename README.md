# Housing Price Analysis and Prediction in New South Wales (NSW), Australia

## 1. Project Overview
This project presents a comprehensive data-driven approach to **analyzing and predicting housing prices** in *New South Wales (NSW), Australia* — one of the country’s most active and high-value real estate markets.  
The study combines **SQL-based exploratory analysis**, **Python-driven statistical modeling**, and **machine learning regression techniques** to identify key determinants of housing prices and construct a predictive framework for valuation.

---

## 2. Objectives
- To analyze spatial and economic patterns influencing property prices across NSW.  
- To construct and evaluate regression models capable of predicting housing prices based on structural and locational features.  
- To demonstrate a full **data science workflow**, including data acquisition, preparation, analysis, visualization, and model validation.

## 4. Methodology
1. **Data Preparation:** Cleaning, transformation, and feature engineering to ensure consistency and comparability.  
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis:** Statistical and spatial investigation to uncover patterns and anomalies.  
3. **Model Development:** Training regression models to estimate price behavior based on predictive attributes.  
4. **Evaluation:** Using metrics such as *R²*, *MAE*, and *RMSE* to assess predictive performance.  
5. **Interpretation:** Identifying the most influential features driving market valuation.

## 6. Key Findings
- Property prices in NSW exhibit significant variation across local government areas (LGAs).  
- **Location** and **land area** are the most critical factors influencing valuation.  
- Machine learning regression models achieved strong explanatory power for housing price estimation.  
- Visualization confirmed clear spatial clustering of high-value regions around urban centers such as Sydney.
